01 — Background

Background Information

PT SMI already had a working business travel system, but it felt cluttered and complicated. The flow between steps wasn't clear, screens were busy, and users often lost track of where they were in the process.

This project is a revamp of that existing system — not a rebuild of the process. The goal was to make the same flow look better, read clearer, and feel simpler to use, so submission, approval, advance payment, and reimbursement all fit together cleanly.

Goal

Turn a cluttered, opaque process into a business travel system that is structured, transparent, and fast — where every request has a clear next step and a status anyone can see.

Scope

Four connected modules

From submission to claim payment — so no data is ever lost between stages.

📄

Travel Authorization

Digital travel letter with destination, duration, purpose, and cost estimate.

✅

Multi-level Approval

Approval routes automatically from supervisor to division head to finance, with delegation.

💰

Advance Payment

Travel advance auto-calculated on per-diem standards and disbursed transparently.

🧾

Reimbursement

Actual-cost claims by uploading receipts, finance verification, trackable payout.

Synthesis

Affinity Mapping

Over 40 notes from the existing system's users clustered into three themes — each pointing at where the current experience feels complicated.

THEME 01Unclear Flow
The order of steps isn't obvious — users aren't sure what comes next.
It takes too many clicks and screens to finish one request.
People lose track of where they are partway through the process.
THEME 02Cluttered Screens
Forms cram too many fields onto one screen at once.
Labels and required fields aren't clear, so mistakes happen.
Key information is buried, making decisions slower for approvers.
THEME 03Low Visibility
Users can't see which stage their request currently sits at.
No clear signal when approval or disbursement has happened.
Status has to be chased manually instead of shown up front.
