Homepage Redesign

A focused redesign concept for a large-scale solar EPC company's homepage — built to communicate engineering expertise, build B2B trust, and turn a single scroll into a confident first impression.

Background

The homepage is where B2B trust is won — or lost in a single scroll.

UVCell Solar delivers Engineering, Procurement & Construction (EPC) solutions for large-scale solar energy projects. In B2B, credibility drives purchasing decisions — and the homepage is the most critical touchpoint.

Reviewing the existing site, I found valuable information buried under a weak visual hierarchy that never quite communicated the company's expertise. I decided to redesign only the homepage — keeping the brand intact while elevating clarity, structure, and trust.

The Challenge

Solving real usability issues
on the original homepage

Problems identified

  • Weak first impression — the hero didn't communicate the value proposition.
  • Dense information lived inside long text blocks that users rarely read.
  • Inconsistent hierarchy — headings, text and stats competed for attention.
  • Low CTA visibility — primary actions weren't prominent enough.
  • Outdated UI that lacked the polish expected of a modern engineering firm.

Goals of the redesign

  • Improve the first impression.
  • Strengthen company credibility.
  • Increase readability and highlight business value.
  • Encourage consultation requests with clearer CTAs.
  • Create a modern enterprise appearance.
