01 — Background

Background Information

Before IIHS had a system, student attendance, grades, and achievements lived in spreadsheets kept separately by different staff. There was no single, current view of which teachers were actively teaching, what they were scheduled to teach, or how students were performing across classes.

This project brings both sides of school administration into one place: a live dashboard, a structured student reporting flow, and a directory of active teachers with their subjects, homeroom, and weekly schedule always kept current.

Goal

Design a school management system where checking a student's progress or a teacher's schedule takes seconds, not a search through spreadsheets — with a live dashboard, structured reporting, and an always-current teacher roster.

Synthesis

Affinity Mapping

Every research note was clustered into three themes that shaped the product direction.

THEME 01Scattered Records
Student results and teacher info live in separate files.
No single source of truth for the whole school.
Manual reconciliation repeated every term.
THEME 02Staffing Visibility
No live view of which teachers are actively teaching.
Homeroom and subject assignments tracked informally.
Staffing changes not communicated in time.
THEME 03Reporting Overhead
Building one report means combining multiple sources.
The same manual effort repeats every single term.
Manual tallying is where most errors creep in.

Scenario

Farah oversees day-to-day school administration — compiling term reports for students, and keeping the teacher roster current as staff join, leave, or change subjects. Both tasks currently mean chasing down separate files kept by different people.

Goals

  • See a live snapshot of the school on login.
  • Generate a student report without combining files.
  • Know instantly which teachers are active.

Frustrations

  • Reports built from four separate spreadsheets.
  • Staffing changes discovered too late.
  • No single source of truth for schedules.
