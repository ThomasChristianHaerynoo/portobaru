01 — Background

Background Information

Finding the right specialist doctor in Indonesia is still a frustrating experience. Patients rely on word-of-mouth, navigate cluttered hospital websites, or wait hours in walk-in queues — only to discover the doctor they needed wasn't available that day. The gap between patient need and accessible healthcare is wide.

TemuDoc was designed to bridge that gap: a mobile-first platform that lets users discover verified specialists by symptom or specialty, view real credentials and patient reviews, and book an in-person or telemedicine appointment in a few taps — no calls, no queues.

Goal

Design a healthcare app where finding a specialist doctor feels as simple and confident as a web search — with transparent credentials, real availability, and flexible booking.

08 — Test

Usability Testing

In order to test the overall quality and ease of navigation throughout the whole design, and to observe areas of errors/difficulties, I went out and had 2 participants interact with my prototype.

Participant Criteria 1

  1. Male Female
  2. Lives in Jakarta and Bandung area
  3. Age 21–35 years
  4. Frequently make medical appointments
  5. Have made medical appointments through a similar application

Participant Criteria 2

  1. Male Female
  2. Lives in Jakarta and Bandung area
  3. Age 21–35 years
  4. Frequently make medical appointments
  5. Have never made a medical appointment through a similar application

Scenario

Sign in using email, and playing the role of Maretha and you will have an appointment with Dr. Chandra Dany. When you're done, we'll ask you a few questions about how you can use the prototype.

Summary

  • Completion Rate: 100%
  • Time on task: 8 seconds
  • For the main goal (appointment with a doctor), participants can understand easily without any obstacles.
  • The UI display is very clean, simple, the color tones are also pleasing to the eye.
  • Participants need an explanation whether this application is a hospital application or a group of doctors who are in one application.
  • Participants really need a CS admin as a front desk to consult before choosing a doctor.
  • Participants thought that the booking date in the form of a calendar would cause the app to lag at the start. But in the end, the participants understood its main function. It may require a clearer color contrast on the calendar so that participants immediately understand its function.
  • Most people mentioned that the application was easy to navigate and didn't struggle to complete the tasks.
