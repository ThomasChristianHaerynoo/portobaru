01 — Background

Background Information

Choosing to study abroad means researching majors, universities, tuition, and admission requirements scattered across dozens of separate institution websites, forums, and agents — with no easy way to compare any of it side by side, and no confirmation once an application has actually been sent.

Edu-GO was designed to bridge that gap: a platform that lets students search programs by major or country, compare shortlisted options clearly, and track every application from submission to decision — kept simple enough to browse on a phone between classes, and thorough enough to finish on a desktop.

Goal

Design a study-abroad platform where finding the right program feels as simple as a web search — with transparent institution credentials, side-by-side comparison, and a clear view of where every application stands.

Synthesis

Affinity Mapping

Every research note was clustered into three themes that shaped the product direction.

THEME 01Fragmented Research
Program information scattered across dozens of separate sites.
Tuition and requirement details are often out of date.
No easy way to compare shortlisted programs side by side.
THEME 02Trust & Credibility
Students are skeptical of listings with no verification signal.
Agent-run sites feel biased toward their own partner schools.
A "verified partner" badge builds immediate confidence.
THEME 03Visibility After Applying
No confirmation once an application is actually submitted.
Students email agents just to check on status.
Multiple applications tracked manually across notebooks or chats.
