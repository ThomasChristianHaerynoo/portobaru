01 — Background

Schedule ahead — scan on arrival

Before VMS, there was no way to register a visit ahead of time. Everything started only when guests had already arrived — confirmation via personal WhatsApp, a physical guestbook, then waiting with no certainty. VMS flips the process: guests schedule days in advance, receive a digital QR pass, and scan it at the lobby for instant check-in.

🎯 Problem

No system existed to register a visit before the day of arrival. Guests showed up unprepared, employees were caught off-guard, and the front desk was overwhelmed with manual guestbook entries during peak hours.

💡 Solution

A web-based pre-registration platform: guests register online, select the PT SMI employee they want to meet, and schedule their visit. Once approved, the system issues a QR pass — scan it at the lobby for automatic check-in.

More than 40 notes from 12 interviews and lobby observations grouped into 5 themes. Dots indicate how frequently each pattern appeared.

Communication
3 notes

Data & Admin
3 notes

Intern data scattered across multiple out-of-sync spreadsheets.

Approval Flow
